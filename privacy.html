Privacy

AQI Alarm shows live air quality for Indian cities in plain language. It is built and run by Aditya Pratap. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

Air quality data comes from WAQI

Readings on AQI Alarm come from the World Air Quality Index project (aqicn.org) through the WAQI data API. When you search for a city or open a city's readings, the city name is sent through this site's API to the WAQI service so it can return current readings.

Location is only used if you ask

If you tap the locate button and allow location access in your browser, your coordinates are sent through this site's API to the WAQI service to find readings near you. Location is never requested in the background and is only used when you tap that button.

Preferences stay on your device

Your recent searches, pinned comparison cities and last-viewed setting are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your device, so the site can remember them for you. They are not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes them.

No accounts, no data selling

There are no accounts, sign-ups or forms on AQI Alarm. Your information is not sold or shared for advertising.
